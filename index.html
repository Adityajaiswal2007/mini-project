The Process

From assessment to achievement

A structured four-step journey designed to maximize your learning outcomes.

01

Create Your Profile

Sign up and tell us your current role, target role, and skill domains you want to develop.

02

Take Skill Assessment

Complete adaptive assessments across your chosen skill domains to establish a baseline competency score.

03

Get Your Gap Report

Receive a detailed skill gap report showing exactly where you stand and what skills need the most attention.

04

Learn and Track Progress

Follow your personalized learning path, complete courses, earn badges, and watch your competency grow.
